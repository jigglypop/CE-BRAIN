## AO.1 — exact separable optimum

For `m=q+1`, cap `rho_cap` implies
`epsilon_k<=epsilon_cap=(rho_cap-1)/2^(2m)`. Feasibility is exactly
`lower_k<=epsilon_cap`. Since the objective is the sum of coordinates, its exact
global maximizer is `epsilon_k^*=min(upper_k,epsilon_cap)`.

## AO.2 — whole selected box

Endpoint vanishing order is independent of amplitude. Hence every point of
`[lower_k,epsilon_k^*]` preserves periodic Cq junctions. With
`epsilon_*=max epsilon_k^*`, radial maximum, gradient, and Lipschitz bounds are
`1+epsilon_*2^(2m)`, `epsilon_*m2^(2m)`, and their sum.
