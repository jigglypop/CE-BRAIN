# Sources lane

Status: COMPLETE

Primary sources checked on 2026-08-25:

1. Huang, Mashour, Hudetz (2023), "Functional geometry of the cortex encodes
   dimensions of consciousness," Nature Communications,
   https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-35764-7 .  The study reports three
   candidate cortical gradients associated with awareness, sensory
   organization, and arousability.  These semantic/neurofunctional gradients
   are not a latent population rank of 3, 4, 5, or 6.
2. Demertzi et al. (2019), "Human consciousness is supported by dynamic
   complex patterns of brain signal coordination," Science Advances,
   https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aat7603 .  Conscious and minimally conscious
   conditions showed richer coordination and transitions than unresponsive
   conditions; no fixed 4--6 latent dimension was established.
3. Stringer et al. (2019), "High-dimensional geometry of population responses
   in visual cortex," Nature, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1346-5 .
   Cross-validated signal spectra were high-dimensional and power-law-like,
   warning against equating all population activity with a tiny fixed rank.
4. Gulati et al. (2022), "Cortical-hippocampal coupling during manifold
   exploration in motor cortex," Nature,
   https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05533-z .  A leave-neuron-out procedure
   selected GPFA dimension 6 for a specific motor-learning analysis, while
   downstream analyses used the top three factors; this is task/method
   specificity, not a consciousness constant.
5. Altan et al. (2021), "Estimating the dimensionality of the manifold
   underlying multi-electrode neural recordings," PLOS Computational Biology,
   https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008591 .  Synthetic benchmarking found
   estimator failures under noise, nonlinear embedding, high dimension, and
   limited data, motivating signal denoising and estimator triangulation.

Source conclusion: current evidence supports structured neural state spaces
whose estimated dimension depends on region, task, state, estimator, noise,
and sampling.  It does not establish a universal 4--6 consciousness dimension.
