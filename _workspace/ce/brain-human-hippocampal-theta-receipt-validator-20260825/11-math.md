# BA-OBS-HPC4 mathematics lane

Status: COMPLETE

The successor changes no estimand. Strict JSON numeric validation rejects `bool`,
string, null, NaN, and infinity before any tolerance comparison. The 599-sample mean
waveform deterministically regenerates early/late/prestim mean P2P on the inherited
shifted masks. A fresh deterministic `aggregate(files)` regenerates all participant
deltas, $D$, shared-PCG64 bootstrap values, LOO, paired sensitivity, and categorical
status lattice. Symmetric `rtol=1e-12, atol=1e-12` comparison is reproducible under JSON
round-trip; categorical structures require exact equality.

Trial-P2P arrays are not derivable from the stored mean waveform and do not feed the
aggregate. Their authority requirement is therefore exact schema, finite numeric type,
and length equal to the corresponding clean count. Clinical reason counts are
channel-trial counts with cap `blocks * len(qc_indices)`; bipolar counts have cap
`blocks`, so p20 legitimately permits `3 * blocks`.

No P0/P1 contract finding remains. Python booleans must be rejected explicitly because
`bool` subclasses `int`.
