# Alternative routes and open boundary

Status: COMPLETE

## Closed

A supplied exact commuting projector and supplied exact complement inverse are
sufficient to certify the circular Riesz projector for defective and
non-rational-spectrum rational matrices.

## Open

- Discovering `P` and `R` from an arbitrary matrix is a separate exact algebra or
  verified numerical problem.
- The sufficient inside/outside norm gates can be conservative; sharper polynomial,
  pseudospectral, or singular-value enclosures are independent successors.
- This theorem does not produce arbitrary-contour quadrature weights or errors.
- Floating/interval witnesses and empirical matrices require provenance and
  simultaneous-coverage contracts.

Failure of a norm gate is inconclusive about the true spectrum.
