# Contract: exact algebraic Riesz projector

Status: COMPLETE

## Objective

Verify a supplied exact Riesz projector for rational matrices without requiring an
eigenvector diagonalization or rational eigenvalues.  The route must admit
defective invariant blocks and rational matrices with irrational eigenvalues.

## Claim ceiling

The projector, complementary centered inverse, center, radius, and scale are exact
supplied inputs.  This is not a projector-discovery algorithm, arbitrary contour
quadrature, floating eigensolver receipt, empirical neural result, or dimension
selection result.
