## 2. Source-to-equation boundary

**[Axiom: physical model selection]** BA-ERC1 chooses the cable--HH level as the first tractable latent law. The PNP system is retained as a higher-fidelity adverse/upgrade route. No exact thin-domain derivation from PNP to the graph PDE is claimed in this run.

**[Derived in this run]** The arbitrary-coordinate expression in equation (4) is an elementary rewrite using $ds=\sqrt g\,dx$; it is not attributed to the cited papers as a new neuron-specific Riemannian law. Yihe and Timofeeva support the metric-graph cable setting, while the conductivity weights and coordinate factors are audited directly in `11-math.md`.

**[Empirical formula]** Hodgkin--Huxley channel kinetics and conductance-based synaptic currents are empirically grounded model families. Their parameter values are preparation-, temperature-, channel-, and cell-type-dependent; BA-ERC1 does not import the squid parameters as mammalian brain constants.

**[Hypothesis]** Replacing a scalar synaptic weight by a bounded functional of normalized causal voltage/current/gate history is the new candidate. The sources motivate its electrical ports but do not prove its biological necessity or its superiority to finite-state synapses.

**[Unfinished]** No primary real-neural dataset, morphology file, intervention, acquisition schema, subject/cell split, or measurement-noise model has been opened. No executable L0 evidence exists at source-lock time; the authorized L0 implementation and receipt are pending.
