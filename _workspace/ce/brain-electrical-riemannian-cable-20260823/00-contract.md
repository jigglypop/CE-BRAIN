## 2. Frozen physical convention

The morphology is a finite metric graph $\Gamma=(\mathcal V,\mathcal E)$. On edge $e$, $s$ is physical arclength, $A_e(s)$ is intracellular cross-sectional area, $P_e(s)$ is membrane area per arclength, $\sigma_{i,e}(s)$ is intracellular conductivity, and $V_e(s,t)=\Phi_{i,e}-\Phi_{e,e}$ is membrane voltage. Positive membrane current is outward; positive applied current is inward. The axial current in the positive-$s$ direction is

$$
I_{\parallel,e}=-\sigma_{i,e}A_e\partial_sV_e.
$$

Every current term in the primary PDE is a membrane current density in $\mathrm{A\,m^{-2}}$. Current and voltage clamps remain typed, separate observables; no coordinate may mix amperes and volts.

`CE_DELTA`: The candidate addition is not a new electrical force. The index $i\leftarrow j$ means presynaptic $j$ drives postsynaptic $i$. It replaces a scalar synaptic weight by a bounded causal history functional

$$

S\!\left(b_{ij}+\langle K_{ij},h_{ij,t}\rangle_{\mathcal H_{ij}}\right),

A physical reference metric, an observation pullback metric, and a derived effective dimension are defined separately; none changes the cable PDE unless a later intervention contract supplies $\Delta F_{\rm CE}$.
