## DI.1 — defective Neumann ball

For DEFCON bound `R_alg^+` and `||E||_F<=epsilon^+`, require
`R_alg^+ epsilon^+<1`. Frobenius dominates operator norm, so every
`I-tE(zI-U)^-1` is invertible. The perturbed resolvent is at most
`R_alg^+/(1-R_alg^+ epsilon^+)`; connected `tE` preserves Riesz rank.
