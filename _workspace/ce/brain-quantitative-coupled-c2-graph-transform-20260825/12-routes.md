# Routes

Status: COMPLETE

| Route | Decision | Reason / reopening condition |
|---|---|---|
| Affine coupled C2 with invariant C2,1 graph class | SELECTED | The full inverse-map Hessian identity closes the exact recurrence. |
| Coupled C2 without map-Hessian moduli | REJECTED | Different preimages leave $D^2f,D^2g$ location changes uncontrolled. |
| Coupled C2 without graph-Hessian modulus | REJECTED when $L_{fy}>0$ | $D^2h(x_1)-D^2h(x_2)$ is uncontrolled; the gate disappears exactly at $L_{fy}=0$. |
| Nonaffine/local coupled C2 | OPEN | Needs variable-base inverse derivatives and explicit boundary mapping. |
| C3 and higher | OPEN | Needs multilinear graph classes and order-wise Faà di Bruno bounds. |
| Empirical neural constants | OPEN | Needs source-locked differentiable dynamics, measurement model, and held-out uniform estimates. |
