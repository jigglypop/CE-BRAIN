## Abstract

## 1. Exact domain equality

If

$$
\phi_t(U_t)\subseteq U_{t+1},
\qquad
\psi_t(U_{t+1})\subseteq U_t,
$$

then applying $\phi_t$ to the second inclusion gives the reverse inclusion.
Therefore

$$
\phi_t(U_t)=U_{t+1}.
$$

The overflow intersection identity strengthens to exact full-graph equality
between consecutive local domains.

## 2. Why the domain margin is zero

For closed balls, exact forward and inverse image suprema must equal their
target radii:

$$
R_{\rm fwd}=r_{t+1},
\qquad
R_{\rm pre}=r_t.
$$

An upper bound above a radius cannot prove inclusion. A claimed exact supremum
below a radius contradicts full image equality because the full ball contains
boundary points. Thus the domain gate is exact and structurally non-robust,
even when the differential class and bunching margins are strict.
