# Alternative routes and open boundary

Status: COMPLETE

## Closed

Any finite predeclared single-winding counterclockwise mesh of exact rational unit
directions, including exact witness construction and maximum-gap chord correction,
is closed conditionally for circular residual/Riesz certification.

## Open

- Arbitrary piecewise-linear, elliptical, or general Jordan contours need their
  own covering geometry and orientation/winding proof.
- Adaptive mesh refinement needs a frozen stopping rule and a receipt showing the
  same interval family was retained.
- Projector quadrature on the arbitrary mesh is not supplied; this theorem proves
  contour exclusion/rank and a perturbation upper, not a new quadrature rule.
- Empirical promotion still needs authenticated matrices and simultaneous coverage.

Mesh failure is a failure of a sufficient certificate, not evidence of a spectral
crossing.
