# Residual routes and falsifiers

Status: COMPLETE

## Admitted componentwise route

Four supplied rational $B_k$ matrices are checked, never trusted. Every
complex magnitude is outward-enclosed after normalization. Both induced norms
must contract strictly, then node inverse uppers and the full-circle chord are
checked in that order.

## Weighted/block candidate

A diagonal similarity weight or block norm could reduce row/column mixing.
Such a weight changes the certificate geometry and requires a frozen choice,
condition reporting, and its own proof. It may not be optimized after seeing a
failed target under the current status.

## Witness-construction candidate

Exact rational witnesses can be derived from declared approximate inverses,
but converting floating numerical solvers into rational witnesses requires a
frozen rounding rule. K1 checks validity regardless of construction; it does
not certify the process that generated $B_k$.

## Falsifiers

- missing/wrong-count/wrong-shape or nonexact witnesses;
- using exact residual without the uncertainty product;
- using lower magnitude or square-root endpoints;
- either induced contraction greater than or equal to one;
- positive nodes but nonpositive chord-corrected lower;
- raw-unit magnitude rounding that changes status under rescaling;
- claiming contour crossing from a failure;
- claiming empirical coverage or consciousness from a deterministic pass.
