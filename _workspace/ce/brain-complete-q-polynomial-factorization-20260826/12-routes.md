# Routes and counterexamples

Status: COMPLETE

## Passing routes

- reducible quartic into two irreducible quadratics;
- irreducible quartic and irreducible degree-eight polynomial;
- repeated irreducible quadratic grouped with multiplicity three;
- nonintegral rational coefficients via primitive lift;
- two irreducible quartic spectral atoms realized by rational normal cycle
  blocks, yielding an exact rank-four projector.

## Refusal and distinction routes

- A candidate-product space larger than the remaining budget is an explicit
  resource refusal, not an irreducibility proof.
- A nonnormal companion realization can have correctly separated eigenvalues
  but fail the stronger exterior-inverse norm sufficient condition.  This does
  not invalidate its exact factorization.
- Complex matrix entries remain refused by characteristic discovery.
- Interval coefficients and empirical provenance remain separate successors.
