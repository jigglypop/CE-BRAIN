The safe empirical statement is only that population ODEs, recurrent dynamics,
latent states and low-dimensional trajectories are established modeling tools.
The affine-fiber construction below is a mathematical model class.  No cited
paper proves that a particular brain circuit has a global affine fiber or that
its fiber variable is a biological folded/nonselected component.
