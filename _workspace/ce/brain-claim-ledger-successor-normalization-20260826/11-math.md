# Status logic

Status: COMPLETE

Let `A -> B` mean that claim `B` proves the mathematical obligation previously
listed as open in `A`, under explicit hypotheses.  Then `A` may be relabeled as
successor-closed exactly when:

1. the successor's hypotheses are retained in `A`'s dependency or ceiling field;
2. no empirical or execution claim is inferred from the theorem;
3. any stronger route not proved by the successor remains explicitly incomplete;
4. a counterexample to the parent would delete, not merely relabel, the parent.

Applied chains include finite-order graph transforms through conditional smooth
and analytic successors, and residual certification through exact witnesses,
supplied similarities, finite predeclared selection, and rational contour meshes.
