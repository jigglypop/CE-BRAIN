# Routes and counterexamples

Status: COMPLETE

## Closed routes

- Sylvester/principal-minor gates verify exact SPD/PSD inputs.
- Exact Gauss–Jordan arithmetic constructs inverse metrics and observed Grams.
- Loewner differences are independently checked at both endpoints.
- Scalar derivative agrees with a closed-form rational expression.
- Zero edge terms realize the equality case.
- A rectangular observation verifies that hard rank equals observation rank.
- Common metric scaling with inverse ridge scaling preserves effective dimension.

## Counterexamples and exclusions

- Coordinatewise weakening is not silently treated as strengthening.
- Indefinite edges, singular baseline metrics, floats, and nonpositive ridge fail.
- Participation ratio can move in either direction under the same edge order.
- A robust `[4,6]` ridge band does not select a signal rank or consciousness dimension.
