# Contract: topological edge disconnection and fixed-contour spectral rank

Status: COMPLETE

## Objective

Separate graph-component disconnection from Hermitian dynamical spectral-rank
change, prove a strict uniform no-crossing condition, and prove that different
endpoint ranks require an intermediate crossing of the fixed contour.

## Acceptance conditions

1. Connected/disconnected adjacencies are exact binary symmetric graphs.
2. Removed adjacency edges correspond exactly to nonzero off-diagonal couplings.
3. Disconnected diagonal spectrum and connected full eigenbasis are exact.
4. A strict Frobenius coupling budget below the disconnected contour gap preserves
   rank throughout the coupling homotopy.
5. Different endpoint ranks imply an intermediate contour crossing.
6. Failed sufficient bounds with equal endpoints remain unresolved.
7. A topology-changing fixture preserves robust rank six.
8. No direct topological-disconnection or consciousness-rank claim is admitted.
