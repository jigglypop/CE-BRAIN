# Routes and counterexamples

Status: COMPLETE

## Closed routes

- Exact graph traversal computes both component counts.
- Coupling support is checked against every removed edge pair.
- The connected orthonormal eigenbasis exactly diagonalizes the connected operator.
- Frobenius squared comparison avoids inexact square roots.
- A scale transformation multiplies the robust squared margin by the scale squared.
- Rank `2->0` verifies the crossing-necessity branch.

## Boundaries

- A failed Frobenius condition is not evidence of crossing.
- Equal endpoint ranks do not exclude an exit-and-reentry crossing.
- Endpoint data establish crossing existence only when ranks differ; they do not
  locate it.
- Topological component change and spectral rank remain distinct claims.
