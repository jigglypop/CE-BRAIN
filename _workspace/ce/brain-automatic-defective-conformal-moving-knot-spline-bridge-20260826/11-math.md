## AD.1 — reference split and automatic witnesses

Set `r_ref=s(rho^-+rho^+)/2`. Complete exact Q factorization groups repeated
factors as primary atoms. The unique strict inside/outside partition gives
coprime factors. Evaluating their Bézout identity at U constructs an exact
commuting idempotent P. Exact inversion of the complemented centered block gives
`R_Q`. Failure of budget, uniqueness, strictness, or inversion yields no claim.

inner/outer envelope gates, rather than the reference-circle result alone,
