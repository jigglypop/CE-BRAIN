# Routes and counterexamples

Status: COMPLETE

## Passing routes

- zero uncertainty reproduces nominal exact projector/rank and zero error;
- a nonzero complex rectangular box preserves rank one with positive robust
  margin and projector bound;
- a Gaussian-rational matrix at non-real center preserves its centered-envelope
  nominal rank across the box;
- raw scaling preserves every normalized receipt.

## Refusal routes

- uncertainty equal to or above the certified margin;
- a nominal eigenvalue on the contour;
- nominal complete-factor candidate-budget refusal despite an independently safe
  interval circle;
- invalid uncertainty schema, partition budget, or mesh contract.

These failures yield no family rank.
