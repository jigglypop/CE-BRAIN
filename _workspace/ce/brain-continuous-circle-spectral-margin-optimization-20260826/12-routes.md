# Routes and counterexamples

Status: COMPLETE

## Passing routes

- a genuine three-dimensional center-real/center-imag/radius box with rank one;
- a degenerate box with exact zero optimality gap;
- raw-unit scaling covariance;
- Gaussian-rational spectrum with non-real centers;
- rank-zero and full-rank frozen targets.

## Refusal routes

- invalid or singular diagonalization witness;
- zero-tolerance request with insufficient cell budget;
- contradictory labels on repeated identical eigenvalues;
- invalid label, interval, radius, tolerance, or budget contracts.

The selected midpoint is validated again by CHSPEC; optimization success alone is
not a projector certificate.
