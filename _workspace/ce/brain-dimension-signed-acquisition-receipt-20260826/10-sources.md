# Sources

Status: COMPLETE

Primary standard:

- S. Josefsson and I. Liusvaara, “Edwards-Curve Digital Signature Algorithm
  (EdDSA),” RFC 8032 (2017), especially §§5.1, 6, and 7.1:
  https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8032.html

RFC 8032 supplies the Ed25519 parameters, verification equation, illustrative
Python arithmetic, and official test vectors. The CE-specific signed-message domain
and successor-chain composition are local definitions, not claims made by the RFC.
