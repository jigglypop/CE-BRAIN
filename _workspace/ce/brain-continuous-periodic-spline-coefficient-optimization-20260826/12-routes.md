# Alternative routes and ceilings

Status: COMPLETE

## Chosen route

Use a finite nonnegative cone generated by higher powers of the already certified
automatic-junction bump.  This preserves exact jets and reduces cap optimization
to independent rational fractional knapsacks.

## Deferred routes

- signed coefficients requiring positivity certificates;
- arbitrary polynomial bases with explicit junction equality elimination;
- nonlinear or empirically learned objectives;
- composition with diagonalized or defective spectral rank bridges;
- data-calibrated uncertainty and the proposed transient dimension 4--6.
