# Stage 2 hypotheses

Status: COMPLETE / OUTCOME_BLIND

- `H_gain`: at the registered sample size and endpoint resolution, the
  state-mean waveforms are compatible with one nonnegative global gain and the
  trial-normalized test has no preregistered evidence of a condition difference.
- `H_state`: after removing trialwise global magnitude, awake and isoflurane
  normalized waveforms differ across currents and the fitted state-mean global
  gain leaves a practically nontrivial residual.
- `H_tension`: evidence is current-specific, statistically weak, or disagrees
  between the direction test and gain-fit residual.

These are operational alternatives for one ordered session. Non-rejection does
not prove gain sufficiency, and rejection may reflect additive-noise/SNR or
directional-dispersion changes as well as mean direction. They are not
anatomical graph hypotheses and do not separate anesthesia from time/order.
