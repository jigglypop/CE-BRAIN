# Stage 2 metrics

Status: COMPLETE / OUTCOME_BLIND

Primary per-current receipts:

1. eligible development and confirmation trial counts by state;
2. `alpha`, the nonnegative least-squares global gain;
3. `R`, relative residual norm after global-gain removal;
4. `D`, trial-normalized waveform distance between confirmation mean unit
   waveforms (direction and resultant length are not separated);
5. the 999-permutation one-sided p-value;
6. exact channel IDs, response grid, waveform shape and finite/norm checks.

Secondary descriptive receipts are channelwise integrated absolute evoked
response, signed peak amplitude and peak latency. They are not multiplicity
corrected and cannot change the frozen decision.
