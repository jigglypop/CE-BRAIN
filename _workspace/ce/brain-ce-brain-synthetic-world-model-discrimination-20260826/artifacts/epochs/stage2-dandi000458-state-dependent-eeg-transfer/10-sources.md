## Primary empirical source

Arena et al., *The perturbational complexity index in rodents*, eLife 12,
e84630 (2023), DOI `10.7554/eLife.84630`:

- https://elifesciences.org/articles/84630v1
- electrical stimulation was biphasic, 200 microseconds per phase, 10--100
  microamperes;
- EEG used 30 electrodes sampled at 2.5 kHz;
- the 0--2 ms artifact interval was replaced by the preceding -2--0 ms segment;
- signals were common-average referenced and bandpass filtered 0.1--100 Hz.

The present endpoint is newly registered and is not claimed to be the paper's
PCI or global-field-power endpoint.
