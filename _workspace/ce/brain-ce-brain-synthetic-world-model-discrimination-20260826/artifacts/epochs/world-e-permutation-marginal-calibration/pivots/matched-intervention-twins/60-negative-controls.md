# Twin confirmation negative controls

Status: COMPLETE

1. For D/E, observed contrasts are permuted only among the eight twins sharing
   the exact same intervention pair, using uniform full-group draws. For
   A/B/C/F/G, twin-independent contrast is the analytic negative control.
2. Equal-arm prediction sets $\widehat D=0$. Its paired and deranged losses must
   be identical, giving advantage zero and p-value one.
3. Common latent initial state, hidden state and innovation schedule are verified
   by simulator receipts but never passed to model features.
4. A deliberate cross-block permutation, pre-serialization confirmation access,
   parent-source mismatch and twin receipt mismatch must fail in focused tests.
