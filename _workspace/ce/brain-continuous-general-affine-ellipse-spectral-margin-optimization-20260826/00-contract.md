# Contract: continuous general-affine ellipse optimization

Status: COMPLETE

## Objective

Extend the continuous oriented-ellipse theorem from orthogonal axes to compact
general orientation-preserving affine axis boxes by optimizing QR shear jointly
with center, positive axes, and orientation.

## Acceptance conditions

1. Use positive-diagonal QR coordinates and prove positive determinant.
2. Derive the exact inverse-coordinate ellipse margin including shear.
3. Prove zero-shear reduction to the stereographic predecessor.
4. Cover every point of a six-variable box with exact-rational outward bounds.
5. Certify a tolerance-global incumbent or fail on the declared cell budget.
6. Require positive separation and exact oblique projector/rank.
7. Preserve spectral-scale covariance and explicit empirical ceilings.

## Ceiling

Spline knots, defective/no-witness noncircular objectives, empirical selection,
consciousness, and dimension 4--6 remain outside.
