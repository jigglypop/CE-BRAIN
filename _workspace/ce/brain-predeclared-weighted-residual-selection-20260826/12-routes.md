# Alternative routes and open boundary

Status: COMPLETE

## Closed route

A finite, exact, canonical menu with development-only unique selection and
selected-only heldout confirmation is closed conditionally.

## Routes not merged into this theorem

- Continuous optimization needs a compact parameter domain, an outward global
  optimizer or exhaustive mesh error bound, and a multiplicity rule.
- Adaptive menu refinement needs a separate outer-data split; otherwise the menu
  is no longer predeclared.
- Block or dense similarities need exact inverse/condition-number enclosures and
  componentwise interval transformation rules.
- Empirical promotion needs a source-locked matrix, estimator, simultaneous
  uncertainty family, immutable split receipt, and execution provenance.

These are independent successors.  Failure of the finite menu is inconclusive
about whether another admissible geometry exists.
